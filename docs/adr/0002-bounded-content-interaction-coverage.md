# M2 proves content units with bounded interaction coverage

Complete content compatibility means that every frozen content unit has evidence at each lifecycle checkpoint appropriate to its type, plus evidence for its declared or observed content interaction contracts. M2 will not attempt an unbounded Cartesian product of every card, relic, Power, enemy, event, and reward; representative and risk-based combinations are required, while uncovered combinations remain explicit gaps. Dynamic registrations are split by registration identity under the fixed version, snapshot, configuration, and runtime conditions. UI, save, map, network, Steam, and cloud behavior remain separate platform capabilities unless they directly carry a content lifecycle.

## Consequences

- A content gate can fail even when the full run reaches a native terminal.
- A content unit cannot pass from load or construction evidence alone.
- The evidence matrix must distinguish unit lifecycle coverage, interaction coverage, and platform capability coverage.
